The oscilloscope was someone's dad's, apparently. That's what the note said. *My dad's — was his work scope. Can it be saved?* Underlined *saved*, which told me something about how worried they were.

Metrix ox-25b, probably mid-nineties. CRT still intact, which was the first good sign. I plugged it in and got nothing — no sweep, no trace, not even the grid. The power light came on, which meant the main transformer wasn't dead, but somewhere between that and the tube, something had quit.

Started at the power supply. The electrolytics on the HV board were bulging, two of them visibly domed, which is exactly what you'd expect from a cap that's been sitting discharged for years and then got hit with voltage. Classic. I pulled them and the values were way off — one reading 40µF that should have been 100. They'd just given up.

Ordered replacements while I worked through the rest of it. Gert was sanding something behind me, that low even sound, and I went through the signal path on the vertical amp looking for anything else tired. Found a coupling cap that measured fine but had a hairline crack in the body — the kind of thing you'd miss if you weren't looking for it. Put it on the replace list.

The new caps came in Thursday. I reflowed a couple solder joints that looked dull while I had the board out, because I was there, because they looked dull.

Powered it up.

The trace came back slow — CRT warming up, beam finding itself — and then it was just there. Sharp, straight, stable. I ran my signal generator through it and watched the sine wave sit on the screen and not move. Calibration was within spec. Forty years old and within spec.

I looked at it for a minute. The beam doing exactly what it was supposed to do.

I wrote on the ticket: *Replaced HV electrolytics and one cracked coupling cap. Reflowed four joints. Calibration checked — within spec. Should be good for another few decades.*

Then I thought about the note. *Was his work scope.* And I thought about how this thing had been on someone's bench for years, measuring things, being trusted, and now it would go back and do that again.

I didn't write that part on the ticket.
