The courtyard sounds fine. I can hear Marta down there with her kid, and the way the kid keeps saying *but why* about something, and it's not annoying, it's the right amount of present. The screen on the south wall is doing its thing. My monitor in the window is open to my island, which I haven't touched in three days.

I just don't feel like being close to any of it right now.

That's the whole thing. Nothing happened. The network work is fine — I'm waiting on a reply from Sven, so there's a gap in my afternoon where I could do anything, and I'm doing this: sitting slightly away from the window, aware of all the things I put in place to feel less alone, feeling alone anyway.

It'll pass. That's not comfort, it's just true. Tomorrow or maybe later today I'll be back by the window, half-watching Marta's kid figure out whatever the kid is figuring out, and it'll feel like enough again.

Right now it doesn't. Right now I'm just in here.
