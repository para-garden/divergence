the monitor's been in the window two years now. i angled it outward because the courtyard was empty and it seemed like a waste. people started waving. i waved back.

the shared screen on the south wall came from the same thought, more or less. i had the parts and the wall had nothing on it. put it up on a saturday, left it open so anyone could cast to it. first week there was an argument about what to put on. i found that good.

i work from here mostly. the courtyard is technically my view and also in some sense my office. not in a way i could explain to someone who needed it explained.
