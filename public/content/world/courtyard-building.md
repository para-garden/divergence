Six floors. Bakery on the corner at ground level, two floors of flats above it, a tailor on the fourth, three more above that. Nothing unusual for the block.

The courtyard's interior — not big, but it gets afternoon light because the south face is only four floors and the building next door doesn't block it. Someone's been growing things in the raised beds along the east wall. Doing well, whatever they are.

You can see the courtyard from the bakery window. You can see the bakery window from the courtyard. People use this.

Looking up from here: plants trailing off a third-floor sill to the west, paint cans on a fourth-floor balcony railing, and on the second floor facing the courtyard, a monitor in the window running what looks like a small village. Been there two years. Nobody's said anything about it. A few people have looked up and waved, though it's not clear at whom.
