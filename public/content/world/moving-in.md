The new person moved in on a Saturday. Kofi held the door because he was coming out with his bike and they were coming in with a lamp. They exchanged the minimal words for that situation. The lamp made it through fine.

Over the next few days the usual calibration happened. Which bin to use. Where to put the bike if you have a bike. Nods in the stairwell that gradually became something you'd call recognizing each other. The couple from the second floor said hello in the way that felt like a standing offer to say hello again if needed. Nobody explained everything at once.

The courtyard was the thing they noticed first — that people used it like it was theirs. Which it was, but it still takes a moment to understand that it's also yours. They ate out there for the first time on a Tuesday, alone, with a book, and Marta's daughter came through on her way somewhere and didn't make anything of a stranger sitting in the courtyard, just passed through.

By the second week they had the corner by the south wall figured out — the light that hits it in the late afternoon. Whether they told anyone about this is unclear. Probably not. It's the kind of thing you find and keep.

The linden was just starting to leaf out. They have no opinions about it yet. They will.
