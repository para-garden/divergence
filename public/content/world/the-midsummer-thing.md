At some point in June the courtyard starts staying up later. Not organized — just the accumulation of long days and warm evenings making it possible to be outside at ten without a jacket, which makes it happen.

The midsummer thing started, as far as anyone can trace, because someone put lights on the courtyard wall six or seven years ago and left them up. The lights are still there. Every June someone tests them and they mostly work, and the ones that don't are replaced by whoever notices first.

This year: the table appeared on the Tuesday before solstice. Food appeared on the table over the course of Wednesday and Thursday as people added things. The linden was at its fullest and the smell was present. Kofi brought the tomatoes from the roof. The Ferreira kids were theoretically supposed to be asleep. Mandarin appeared on the wall at some point and stayed for an hour.

Nobody planned it. Nobody is in charge of it. It's in the calendar system because Noa added it two years ago when someone asked if the tool could do recurring events, and she added the midsummer gathering as a test case and it stayed. People mark themselves as coming or not coming or maybe. The event itself is whatever it turns out to be.

This year it went until one in the morning. That's late for the courtyard. The linden smell was still there at one. Renata had gone up at eleven; she'd been the last of the older ones. The Ferreira kids really were asleep by then. It was mostly people in their thirties, plus Jared, plus Yusuf, finishing wine and talking about something that had started as a specific topic and become a general conversation.

Somebody has to turn the lights off. This is always the last thing.
