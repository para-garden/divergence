She needed to know how to plaster a wall. Not a lot of wall — a patch about thirty centimeters across where a hook had pulled out and taken some of the plaster with it. She'd watched something about it. The watching didn't fully resolve the question.

She posted on the forum. Three people replied. One of them had done it last year and sent photos. One of them offered to come over. One of them sent a link to something she'd already seen.

The person who offered to come over did come over. It took forty minutes. She watched and also helped in the sense of holding things and mixing things and passing things. He explained what he was doing as he went, in the way of someone who learned by watching and hasn't had to explain it much, so the explanation was practical and occasionally incomplete but she could ask questions. She asked questions.

The patch dried. It's not invisible — the texture is slightly off, she'll need to do another pass, he told her this. But it's closed and it's stable and the hook is back and she knows how it works now.

He didn't want anything for it. She offered coffee and he had some. They talked for twenty minutes about the building situation, which they both have opinions about, and then he went home.

She knows how to plaster now. Not perfectly. Enough to do it again and do it better. That's how it goes.
