My window faces the courtyard of the building across the way. Not my building's courtyard — I'm not in a courtyard building. Just the one you can see from here.

There's a woman who hangs laundry in a consistent order. Tops first, from left to right. Bottoms after, also left to right. She's been doing this since I moved in. I don't know what order feels natural to her versus what's just efficient versus what she learned from someone, but it's the same every time.

There's a man who eats lunch outside regardless of weather. If it's raining he has a jacket. If it's cold he has two jackets. He reads. He finishes the book at some point — I noticed because one day the book was gone and he was just eating. A week later he had a different one.

Two kids who were small when I moved in are not small anymore. They still use the courtyard the same way — in the sense that whatever they were doing then, they're still doing a version of it now. Running through and then not being there and then running through again. Now they run farther before coming back.

I've watched this courtyard for years. I've never met any of them. If I moved out tomorrow I don't know that they'd notice the change in which window was lit. That seems fine. Some things are just what you see from where you are.

The man with two jackets has not been out for lunch in a while. I find I've noticed this.
