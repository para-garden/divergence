Bob put it up on a saturday. South-facing wall, good sight lines from the benches and from the raised beds. He left it open — anyone in the building can cast to it, no password, no schedule.

The first week there was an argument. Not a bad one. Two people wanted music and one person wanted to put on a film and nobody was wrong exactly, they just all wanted the courtyard at the same time in different ways. It got resolved the way these things get resolved: someone went first and someone else waited and eventually they worked out a loose understanding about evenings versus afternoons that nobody formally agreed to but everyone follows.

The screen has been there three years. It runs music most afternoons. On Friday evenings in summer someone usually puts on a film and people bring things to eat. This was not planned. It happened twice and then became the thing that happens.

Sometimes it's just showing whatever Bob was last watching, unattended, because he forgot to turn it off. Nobody changes it immediately. There is a grace period of approximately twenty minutes, which is also unspoken.
