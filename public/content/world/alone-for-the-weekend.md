Her people are away this weekend. Not together — separately away, various reasons, just coincidence that the weekend opened up empty. She noticed on Thursday with something like relief, which surprised her slightly, and then didn't surprise her.

Saturday she did almost nothing. Ate what she wanted when she wanted it. Read in the positions that are comfortable for one person and not for two. Walked in the afternoon with no one to consult about direction, which meant she went somewhere she'd been meaning to go for months — down along the old canal section, not dramatic, just a canal, but she'd been saying she'd go and now she went.

She cooked a proper dinner for herself and ate it slowly and didn't eat it standing up at the counter, which is what she sometimes does when people are around and dinner is a social event and the actual eating is secondary. Alone, dinner is dinner. She tasted it.

Sunday she stayed in most of the morning. Read some more. There was a point around eleven where she could feel herself getting restless, and she noticed the restlessness, and then it passed. The flat felt larger than usual in the way that space feels different when there's no one else in it.

By Sunday evening she was ready for people again. The weekend had a shape: from relief to genuine enjoying to restlessness to resolution, and back around to wanting her people. That's the whole circle. She found it satisfying to have gone around it.

She sent a few messages. Two replies came in. She made tea.
