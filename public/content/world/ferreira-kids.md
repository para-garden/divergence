There are three of them and they operate as a unit during the day and as separate people at meals. The oldest one, Clara, is twelve now and sometimes doesn't come out with the younger two, which the younger two have had to adapt to. They've adapted. They added a kid from the building next door who had been watching from the gate.

The courtyard is their geography. They know which corner gets sun at which time of day. They know the bench is not really a bench, it's a boundary. They know the linden is good for low branches and better for watching things fall off the high ones in October.

Games that don't have names. Not tag, but a thing that involves running and zones and rules that evolve mid-game and can't be explained to someone not already in it. Adults have stood at the edge of this game and been unable to determine its logic. The kids haven't noticed this.

Marta's daughter sometimes joins. She's younger and allowed because Clara decided she was allowed, which is how it works. Bea comes when she's not in the workshop. The kid from the building next door is now just *the fourth one* and his name is Sami.

The sunflowers on the roof are partly theirs — they did the planting in May and check on them regularly via Kofi's stairwell, which Kofi has made no rules about. In September they watched Kofi harvest something and he showed them how to tell if a tomato is actually ready, and Clara retained this information for a purpose that hasn't been clear yet.

They'll be older next year. They already were older than last year. The courtyard won't notice.
