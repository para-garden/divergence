The kid had a stick and was using it to poke at something in the gap between the courtyard bricks — something dead, maybe, or just brown.

"Why do things dry out?"

Marta was sitting on the low wall eating the last piece of bread from the bag they hadn't sold. "What do you mean."

"Like — why does it get dry. Where does the wet go."

"Into the air."

The kid poked the thing again. "But the air already has wet in it."

"It has room for more."

This seemed acceptable. A pause. Then: "How does it know there's room."

Marta thought about this. "I don't think it knows. It just — moves toward where there's less of it."

"Always?"

"Mostly."

The stick went still. "Does that happen to people."

Marta looked over. The kid wasn't asking anything alarming, just following the logic somewhere. "Sometimes," she said.

"You moved here."

"I did."

"Was there less of you somewhere else."

Marta was quiet for a second. "More like — less room. Here had more room."

The kid seemed satisfied by this and went back to the thing in the bricks. Marta finished the bread.
