Ground floor, street-facing, the door propped open in warm weather. Daphne has the right half — a long bench, a cabinet of components sorted by type, a lamp on a flexible arm that she moves around constantly. Gert has the left — a bigger bench, clamps along the wall, the smell of wood glue that doesn't go away.

They have been sharing the space for four years. They do not talk much. This is the arrangement and it suits them both. If one of them needs to make a phone call they go outside. If something needs two pairs of hands they ask. Otherwise: the sound of work, the radio on low, occasionally the sound of the other one swearing quietly at something.

The window faces north. The light is even all day, which is why they took it. Neither of them wanted to work somewhere the light changed.
