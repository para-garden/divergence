The cutting on the west sill has been here longer than anything else in the flat. Six years. It's leggy and not particularly attractive but it came from the old place and it took, so it stays.

The others accumulated because spots kept presenting themselves. The ledge was the right depth for the window box. The trailing one found its way toward the courtyard and I let it. I wasn't planning a collection. I was just paying attention to where the light landed.
