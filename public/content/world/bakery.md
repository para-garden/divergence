The smell gets out through the ventilation. Not far — the block turns — but if you come from the east side you catch it before you see the sign.

The sign is hand-painted tile. Someone renewed it twice since the building went up, matching the original imperfectly both times. Visible if you look. Most people don't.

Four tables inside. Three fit two, one fits four if everyone's okay being uncomfortable. They are. The window table is the one to get — light's worse but you can see the courtyard from there.

Coffee's good. Bread's better. Croissants on Tuesdays and Fridays. Go before nine-thirty or they're gone.

Marta's been doing the morning shift for eleven years. Knows your order by the end of the first week. Doesn't make a thing of it.
