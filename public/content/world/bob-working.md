he works from here mostly. this is all he can say about it without it getting complicated.

what he does: something with networks, the kind of thing that runs underneath things so other things can work. he doesn't usually explain it to people unless they ask specifically and mean it, because explained casually it sounds either boring or abstract and it's neither, it's just not visual in a way that makes sense in casual conversation.

the courtyard is technically his view. he realized after about two years that it's also his calibration — he looks at it when he's stuck, not for inspiration, just to look at something that has a different rhythm than whatever he's doing. the people coming and going, the light changing, the linden in whatever state it's in. then he looks back at the screen and things are sometimes different.

the monitor in the window started as an experiment. people started waving and he waved back and it became a thing. the shared screen on the south wall came from the same impulse: this space could have something in it. he put the thing there and left it open and now it does what it does, which is mostly be available.

he has a meeting at two. before that: the problem he's been thinking about since monday, which may have resolved itself overnight. this happens sometimes — you go to sleep with something unresolved and wake up knowing the shape of the solution, and the night did the work of finding it without you. he's learned not to try to force this. you do the work and then you leave it alone and sometimes it arrives.

kofi came through the courtyard a few minutes ago with his bike. lila was in the courtyard for a while, looking at the paving stones. the cat has been on the wall since nine.

the problem may have resolved. he'll look at it now.
