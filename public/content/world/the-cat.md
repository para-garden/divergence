The cat appeared on the courtyard wall in October and has been appearing on the courtyard wall on and off since. Not every day. Not on any schedule that anyone has worked out. It's ginger, medium-sized, in good condition, not particularly interested in making contact with people but not alarmed by them either.

Renata has seen it from her kitchen window often enough to have opinions about its habits. It comes in the morning sometimes and in the afternoon sometimes and occasionally in the evening. She's never seen it two days in a row.

It is not a stray in the sense of looking hungry or frightened. It may belong somewhere, or it may have arrangements with several buildings. This is the characteristic situation of urban cats: the accounting is murky.

The Ferreira kids tried to make friends with it in November. The cat considered their approach, held still while Clara extended her hand, sniffed once, and then left. Clara reported this as a success. Her analysis: the cat touched it. Technically true.

Bob saw it on the courtyard screen once — someone had pointed a camera at the wall, accidentally or on purpose, and there was the cat. He left it on the screen. Several people watched it do nothing for about four minutes. Consensus: good content.

The cat has a life that the courtyard is one small part of. This seems like the right amount of involvement.
