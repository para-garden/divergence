Nothing particular went wrong. That's the thing about this kind of stretch — it doesn't have a cause you can point to and fix. It's just the months when everything is a little more difficult than usual and you're a little less yourself.

She got through it in the way you get through these stretches: mostly by going through them. She talked to people when she could. She went quiet when she couldn't. She saw the doctor once, which was useful, not because anything was diagnosed but because having someone ask careful questions and take it seriously is useful, and she left with some things to try and did try them with mixed results, which is about what she'd expected.

What she didn't have: the material calculation underneath the hard months. The question of whether this would cost her something professionally, whether she could afford to be less than her best. She was less than her best for three months and her best was there when she came back. The ground was the ground.

By spring it had mostly lifted. Not because spring cured anything. Just: these things have a shape and this one's shape was about three months, and then the shape ended. She knew enough to not trust the first good days fully, and then there were enough good days in a row that she could trust it.

She still doesn't know why those months were those months. That's fine. You don't always know. She came through and there was her life waiting for her, intact, which is what you need from a life when you're going through something.

She'd tell someone if they asked. She doesn't go around telling people. It's hers.
