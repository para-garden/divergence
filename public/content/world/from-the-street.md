From the street, late, the building is several lit windows and one that isn't.

Third floor left: the plants on the sill visible if you know to look. The trailing one is making its way toward where the light comes from.

Fourth floor: the balcony wall is visible from below in the day — whoever is walking past looks up sometimes, the way you look up at things that are worth looking at. At night you can't see the wall itself but you can see the light in the window behind it.

Second floor center: the monitor, facing the courtyard rather than the street, sometimes showing something. From the street you can only see the glow of it.

The courtyard is through the archway. If you're passing on the street you might catch a slice of it — the screen on the south wall, the bench, the linden in summer. Late at night the courtyard is usually quiet. The lights Kofi put in the linden frame in June are off.

There are other buildings on this street. Similar buildings, different insides. The bakery on the ground floor is dark. It will be lit at five-thirty.

The person walking past is going somewhere or coming from somewhere. They might live here or not. The building doesn't notice the person walking past. The person might notice the building or they might not — it's night, it's late, they have somewhere to be or they're close enough to home that their attention is already there.

The plants are still on the sill. The monitor is probably still on.
