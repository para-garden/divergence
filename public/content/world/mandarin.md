Nobody knows where the name came from. One day someone posted the photo on the forum and captioned it with the name and now that's what it is. The cat doesn't know its name. This is irrelevant.

Mandarin appeared again on the wall last Tuesday. Renata saw it from her window at six in the morning. Bob saw it at noon. Kofi saw it when he was coming in with his bike in the evening and it was already leaving. These were three separate sightings of what was probably the same visit or possibly three different visits, the accounting on Mandarin's schedule being unclear.

Clara tried again in March. She approached from the north side of the wall, slowly, with the back of her hand presented in the way she'd researched. Mandarin allowed the approach, sniffed the hand, then turned and walked in the opposite direction with the specific cat quality of decisive departure. Clara remains convinced this is progress.

Someone asked on the forum if anyone knew who the cat belonged to. Forty-three replies. Consensus: Mandarin belongs to herself, and also to the neighborhood, and also specifically to the wall, which is a good wall for a cat and has been for some time.

She is occasionally on the rooftop. Kofi has seen her there twice. She was not interested in the tomatoes. She was interested in something below the tomatoes that nobody else could see.

The courtyard has a cat now, more or less. The courtyard has had cats before. It will have cats after. This one has a name.
