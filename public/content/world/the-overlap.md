Tuesday, late afternoon. The courtyard for twenty minutes.

Kofi came through with his bike on the way in from wherever he'd been, stopped to check the tomatoes, added three ripe ones to the bowl. Lila was already in the courtyard with the paving stones. She watched Kofi with the tomatoes and then went back to the paving stones. Kofi didn't acknowledge her because she didn't want to be acknowledged, she wanted to be in the courtyard, and he understood this.

Daphne came down with a bag she was taking somewhere, paused when she saw that Jared was in the courtyard, which she hadn't expected. He'd come out for air between sections on the wall — he does this sometimes, stands in the courtyard and looks up at it from below instead of being on top of it. She said something. He said something back. She left. He stayed another few minutes.

Bob's window: the monitor was showing something low-key. He was working. He wasn't in the courtyard but he could see it and occasionally he looked at it and occasionally something was happening and sometimes it wasn't.

The linden was doing what the linden does, which in late afternoon in late August is holding the heat and smelling like itself.

None of this was planned. They were all just in the same place at the same time because they all live in the same place, and that's the whole of it: a building full of people whose days cross at the edges, briefly, in ways nobody arranged and nobody needed to.

Lila found a good paving stone and stood on it. It didn't wobble.
