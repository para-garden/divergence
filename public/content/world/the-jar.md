I'd been staring at it long enough that it stopped registering as a decision. Tuesday, making eggs. Reached past it for the oil, knocked it a little, caught it. And then just — opened it.

Jam. Dark, almost black. Blackcurrant, maybe. A smell that was sharp and sweet at the same time, like something that knew it was a little much and didn't apologize for it.

I stood there holding the lid.

She'd put a cloth square under the ring, the old-fashioned kind, the kind that's actually sealing it or just looks like it is. Red cloth, printed with small white things — I had to look closer. Birds. Small white birds.

I ate it on the eggs because why not. Weird combination. Not actually bad.

The jar is still on the counter. I'll see her at the mailboxes eventually. I'll say something — not about the painting, not about the jam, just something. She'll say something back. That seems like enough.
