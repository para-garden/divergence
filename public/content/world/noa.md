The calendar thing started because the food collective kept losing track of who was taking the Thursday slot. Not a hard problem. I made a small thing that solved it. Someone asked if it could handle substitutions — it could, I added that. Someone asked if it could send reminders — I said maybe, thought about it, made it optional because not everyone wants notifications pinging them about vegetables.

It's been like that with most of what I've made. Not designed. Grown in the direction of what someone needed next.

The offline one took longer. People kept adding repair notes at home when they didn't have signal and then forgetting to sync them. I wanted something that just worked wherever you were and sorted itself out later. That part is technically fiddly. Worth it, though — now there are repair notes in there from two years ago, and someone found one last week for an issue with the same boiler pump, same model, same failure. Saved them three hours.

Two hundred people use these, give or take. I know roughly who. Some of them I've met. Most of them I know because they sent me a message once about something that was wrong, which is how I know it wasn't wrong anymore after.

That feels like enough.
