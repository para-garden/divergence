It's in the middle of the street. Not the side, not a planter box at the kerb — the middle, where a car would have gone. Someone planted it maybe fifteen years ago and it took and now it's just there. The street goes around it. In October it has persimmons.

Nobody knows whose idea it was. The leading theory is a woman named Oksana who lived in the building on the left until about twelve years ago, but this is unverified and she's not around to ask. The other theory is that it seeded itself, which is botanically unlikely for a persimmon but gets repeated anyway.

In October people take the persimmons. Not all of them — there are always some left. The ones at the top stay until they go soft and fall.
