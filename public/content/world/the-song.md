Noa was debugging something unimportant when the song came on — a track she hadn't heard in maybe three years, something a friend had sent in a message she'd scrolled past and then somehow saved. She didn't move to turn it up. Just let it happen.

Outside: pigeons doing the thing pigeons do. Someone across the courtyard with their window open, making what smelled like onions.

The song was good. It was just good. She sat there with her hands in her lap and let it finish.
