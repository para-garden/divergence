Eleven years on this block. I took over from a man named Georg who wanted to go to Portugal. He gave me a week of handoff and I've been here since.

The hours are the hours. I don't mind them. You get the city before it's decided anything.

I make sourdough, three croissant varieties, rye, a sandwich loaf, and whatever's seasonal. Right now: a plum thing that's been working. I don't have a name for it.

If you come in regularly I know what you get. I won't announce this. Just tell me if it changes.

The oven was out last spring. Daphne from the third floor fixed it. I gave her bread. It was a good transaction.
