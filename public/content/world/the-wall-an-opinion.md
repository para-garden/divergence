The note appeared on the corkboard by the mailboxes on a wednesday:

---

**re: the fourth floor balcony wall (courtyard side)**

Hi — I don't know which unit this is. I've been living in 2B for six years and I used to eat lunch in the courtyard most days. The mural, or whatever it is, has been changing for three years now and I want to just say honestly: I find it hard to look at. It's not finished, or it's always being finished, I can't tell which. The colors are very loud. I'm not saying it shouldn't exist. I just notice I've been eating lunch inside more, and I wanted to say something rather than just keep quietly minding it.

— Petra (2B)

---

There were two responses, also handwritten, pinned below it by the time Jared saw it.

The first:

*Petra — I hear you. I'm on the third floor, I see it every time I step outside. I'll be honest, when he started I thought the same thing. Now I kind of look for what changed. It's not restful. But I don't know if restful is what he was going for. — Miriam (3C)*

The second, in different handwriting, a little cramped:

*That's mine. Fourth floor, second from the end. I'm Jared. I'm sorry it bothers you. I don't know how to make it quieter without making it not itself. I'm not trying to be loud at you. If you want to come up and see it close sometime you can — it's different up close. No obligation. — J*

Petra did not come up. Jared kept painting. He and Petra said hello at the mailboxes, the way you do. It wasn't a rift. It also wasn't resolved. She still mostly ate lunch inside.
