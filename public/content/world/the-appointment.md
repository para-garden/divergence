Something felt off. Not dramatic — just a thing in her shoulder that had been there for two weeks, a tightness that didn't resolve on its own. She made an appointment.

She went on a Wednesday because Wednesday had a gap. The office is four streets over. She's been to this same office probably twenty times over the years, same address, the furniture slightly different, the doctor once and then a different doctor after he moved and then the current one. She likes the current one. She doesn't think about this often.

The appointment was twenty minutes. The shoulder was a muscle thing — she'd been carrying a bag wrong for months, which seems exactly the kind of thing you'd carry wrong for months without noticing. She got the exercises. She'll do them.

She left and walked to the market because it was there. Bought something for dinner. The shoulder still hurt on the way home and she thought about the exercises and thought: Wednesday. She'd start Wednesday.

Not a complicated day. Not even a day she'll remember specifically. She noticed something felt off, she went, now she knows what it is. That's the whole shape of it.
