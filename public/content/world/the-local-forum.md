The neighborhood forum has been running for nine years under four different names and two different platforms. The current version is fine. It's not slick and it's not supposed to be. It looks like what it is: a page where people in a specific geographic area can post things.

Current active threads:

The thing about the elm on the corner, which has a fungal issue and three people with strong opinions about treatment. The conversation is detailed, specific, and occasionally sharp when one of the three realizes the other two have been talking to each other without them. The elm will probably be fine.

Someone asking if anyone has a stand mixer they'd loan for Saturday. Two offers within an hour.

A long thread about the road resurfacing schedule, which has seventeen replies and has migrated from the practical question about scheduling to a philosophical discussion about road surface materials that has departed from anything actionable but is genuinely interesting if you like that sort of thing. Four people apparently do.

The noise complaint about the band practice on Wednesdays, which has been resolved — they moved to Thursdays — but the thread has remained active because people keep replying to say they don't actually mind the Thursdays either.

A photo of the cat on the courtyard wall, captioned only with the cat's name, which is apparently Mandarin and which nobody agreed to but everyone is now using.

Drama exists. It's the proportional drama of people with overlapping lives who have differing opinions about elm trees. Nobody is defending their survival. The stakes are: the elm, the stand mixer, the noise on Thursdays.
