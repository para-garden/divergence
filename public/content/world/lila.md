The linden is hers. This is her understanding. She has explained this to people but they keep not agreeing, which she finds puzzling.

She is four. She lives on the first floor. The courtyard is accessible from her front door in a number of steps she knows exactly — seven, six if she stretches. She has strong opinions about the bench (good), the paving stones (some good, some bad, the bad ones wobble), the cat Mandarin (best), and the people in the building (mostly fine, Kofi is very tall, the woman with the plants is nice, she gave Lila a small pot with something growing in it that Lila has been watering correctly).

Adults do things that don't make sense for reasons that later turn out to be about time, which is something she is still working out. You can go to the courtyard, but then you have to come back. You can pet the cat, but then the cat leaves. Things end and then they happen again. She finds this mostly acceptable.

She knows things. She knows the smell of the bakery before it is visible. She knows the crack in the third paving stone from the archway. She knows that Tomás makes the cups and that the cups come from the place with fire that she is not allowed to go into yet but has looked into from the outside. She knows the linden drops seeds in a specific way and she has been collecting them since October with a purpose she hasn't fully articulated.

Her mother says she remembers everything. She doesn't know why this would surprise anyone. Things happen, you keep them.

The linden seeds are in a box. The box is under the bed. She has a plan.
