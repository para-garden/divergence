The kettle takes a while. I stand at the counter while it heats and look at the grout samples I left on the table last night — three whites, one that's not quite white. I should get those back to Hana by Thursday.

The flat is small enough that I know where everything is without looking. Mug on the hook. Coffee in the jar on the left. The jar on the right is something Petra brought over months ago and I still haven't opened it and now it feels like I can't.

There's a brush on the windowsill I forgot to clean properly. The bristles dried a little stiff at the end. I'll deal with it later.

The light comes in from the east for maybe an hour in the morning. Gets the table, gets the floor tiles — I installed them myself, took a long weekend, slightly over-spaced the grout lines in one corner but nobody's ever going to notice except me. I notice every time.

I take my coffee and stand by the window for a minute before I go out. Not looking at anything specific. Just standing.

Then I go out.
